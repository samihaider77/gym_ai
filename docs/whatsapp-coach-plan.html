Coach on WhatsApp

Members link their WhatsApp number once. After that they can ask for today's workout, their diet targets, or send a meal photo straight from WhatsApp, without opening the app.

Yes, it is possible, and the official way is free to start.

Use Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. Each business number gets 1,000 free reply messages a month. That covers testing and roughly the first 20 active members. After that, each reply costs about US$0.015 in Pakistan. Most messages need no AI. When AI is needed, it uses free models with a fallback chain: Gemini, then OpenRouter :free, then Groq.

  • Recommended path: build Level 2 (command bot) first, then Level 3 (AI chat and meal photos).
  • Telegram: build the chat core so it doesn't depend on WhatsApp. A Telegram bot then comes almost free and costs nothing per message.
  • Avoid unofficial WhatsApp libraries (WhatsApp Web clones). They break WhatsApp's terms and the number gets banned.

What "free" really means

WhatsApp cost, worked out

Meta changed its prices on 1 October 2026. Replies to a member who wrote in the last 24 hours ("service messages") used to be unlimited and free. Now each number gets 1,000 free a month and pays the utility rate after that. Messages members send to us are always free.

Typical active member: 3 questions × 4 gym days × ~4 weeks ≈ 50 replies/month Free allowance: 1,000 ÷ 50 ≈ 20 active members free Above that: 50 × $0.015 ≈ $0.75 per member per month Morning reminder: 1 template × ~20 days × $0.015 ≈ $0.30 per member per month

Two ways to keep it low:

  • One rich reply instead of several short ones. The whole day's plan goes in one message.
  • Interactive buttons and lists ("Log set", "Swap exercise", "Tomorrow"). Members tap instead of typing, which means fewer follow-up messages.

For the gym-owner model, about $1 per member per month fits inside a paid "AI coach" add-on.

AI, kept free

Most messages never touch an LLM

A plain router handles about 80% of messages, such as today, diet, done, weight 78 and button taps, straight from the database. That is instant, free and never wrong. Only free text goes to an LLM. The LLM calls our own functions (get_today_workout, get_day_nutrition, log_set, log_meal, swap_exercise), so it can't invent a plan the member doesn't have.

All providers below speak the same OpenAI-style chat API. One small client tries them in order and moves to the next on a quota or timeout error.

Linking and privacy

Connect once, stop any time

  • Linking with a one-time QR code: Profile → "Connect WhatsApp" shows a QR code made just for that member. It encodes wa.me/<bot number>?text=LINK 482913.
    • On a laptop: the member scans the QR with their phone camera. WhatsApp opens with the message already typed, and they tap Send.
    • On a phone: the same link shows as a "Open WhatsApp" button, because you can't scan your own screen.
    • One-time: the code works once and expires after 10 minutes. After that the QR greys out and shows a "New code" button. Only a hash of the code is stored.
    • Safety: the number is taken from the message itself, so a member never types it and we never guess it. Scanning someone else's QR links only the scanner's own number, and only to that account until the code is used. The member then sees "Linked to •••• 4821" in the app and can disconnect if it's wrong.
  • Gym front desk (later): each gym gets a printed QR for joining. It opens the app sign-up with the gym preselected, and the member then links WhatsApp with their own one-time QR.
  • Unknown numbers: they get only a short "connect from the app" message and see no data.
  • Opting out: STOP unlinks the number and stops all messages, as WhatsApp policy requires. The Profile page shows the link and a Disconnect button.
  • Data: photos are not stored, the same as Meal Snap. Phone numbers are stored hashed, and only the last 4 digits are shown. Gym owners never see a member's chat text, only usage counts.
Build order

Phases

Week 1

Setup and linking

Meta developer app with the free test number. Webhook verify and signature check. chat_links migration. Profile "Connect WhatsApp" card with a one-time QR code (drawn in the browser, so no QR service is needed). LINK, STOP and help.

Week 2

Level 2 commands

today, tomorrow, week, diet, done/log set, weight, with Roman Urdu synonyms (aaj, khana). Reply buttons. Unit tests per command, the same style as test_meal_snap.py.

Week 3

Meal photos

Download the WhatsApp image, run the existing Meal Snap analysis, reply with the items, and log on "save". Voice note → Gemini transcription → the same router.

Week 4

Level 3 AI chat

LLM provider chain with tool calling, daily per-member cap, and a command-menu reply when all free quotas run out. Telegram channel on the same core.

Later

Level 4 and gyms

Meta Business verification. A gym-branded number per gym. Approved reminder templates. Usage counts in the admin panel so the gym owner can see what the add-on is worth.

Before going live

What you need to set up

  • A Meta (Facebook) developer account and a WhatsApp Business app. The free test number is enough for building.
  • For real members: a phone number that isn't already on WhatsApp, Meta Business verification, and a payment method on the account. Without a payment method, replies stop once the 1,000 free ones are used.
  • A display name and profile picture approved by Meta (LiftAndPlate).
  • Free keys: OpenRouter and Groq, plus the Gemini key you already have.

Risks to keep in mind

  • Prices and free tiers change. Meta changed prices twice in 2026, and free AI caps move often. Keep both behind small adapters so a provider can be swapped in a day.
  • Serverless timeouts. Meta expects a fast 200 from the webhook. A photo analysis takes 5–20 s, so send "checking your photo…" first and do the work in the background, or move to a small always-on worker later.
  • Health advice. The AI must not give medical advice. Pain or injury questions get a "see a doctor or your trainer" line plus a safe exercise swap.
